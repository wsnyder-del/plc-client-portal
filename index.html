Agreement for Services
This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into and is effective as of the date of signing below between Pioneer Legal Consulting and the signing solar homeowner(s).
1. SCOPE OF SERVICE COMMITMENT: In exchange for the Service Fee paid under the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Pioneer Legal Consulting commits to use its best efforts to facilitate a favorable resolution to the Client's existing solar purchase agreement. A favorable resolution would be one or more of the following:
Thoroughly reviewing your solar purchase agreement and financing documentation and having our chosen firm write demand letters to either eliminate or significantly reduce your payment obligations under your existing solar purchase agreement;
Having our chosen firm write letters and/or initiate filings to remove any UCC lien filings arising out of the solar purchase agreement from your property;
Negotiating on your behalf to allow you to keep the solar equipment that has been installed.
2. ARBITRATION AND/OR LITIGATION: Arbitration and/or litigation against the solar company and/or the finance company may become an option and/or necessary depending upon the specific facts of your case. Pioneer Legal Consulting, LLC will advocate on your behalf up to selecting outside counsel to represent Client in formal arbitration or litigation proceedings. If arbitration and/or litigation becomes an option or is necessary, Pioneer Legal Consulting, LLC will take the necessary steps to continue to advocate on your behalf, up to engaging outside counsel on your behalf on a separate contingency fee employment contract. In the event outside counsel is engaged, any contingency fee owed to counsel will likely be covered in a statutory manner pursuant to applicable consumer protection laws, which often provide for the recovery of attorney's fees from the opposing party as part of a successful claim. This means that attorney's fees already paid in are often added into the total recovery sought from the solar company and/or finance company, and are not deducted from the monies recovered on your behalf. As a result, Client can expect that in many cases legal fees incurred through outside counsel will be built into the recovery rather than subtracted from Client's proceeds. Any costs or fees associated with formal arbitration or litigation proceedings beyond PLC's scope of service will be disclosed to Client in writing before being incurred.
3. SERVICE FEE: In consideration of the services rendered by Pioneer Legal Consulting, the Client agrees to pay Pioneer Legal Consulting the agreed-upon flat fee as reflected in the Service Fee field above.
4. THIRD PARTY ASSOCIATES: Pioneer Legal Consulting has secured the services of third-party experts to provide the additional education and resources necessary to fully navigate the Client through various issues ancillary to the services performed under this Agreement.
5. LEGAL DISCLAIMER: Pioneer Legal Consulting is not engaged in the practice of law. The client acknowledges that Pioneer Legal Consulting will operate as case manager and paralegal to the attorney Pioneer Legal Consulting selects to represent them. Neither Pioneer Legal Consulting nor the firm being referred to represents a guarantee that the Client will receive a recoupment of funds from the solar sales company or finance company.

(a) Definition of Resolution. "Resolution" means a written, documented outcome that permanently modifies, cancels, or reduces Client's financial obligation or contract status with the subject lender or solar installer.
(b) Refund Upon Conclusion Without Resolution. (c) Conclusion of Engagement. The engagement is not concluded, and no refund obligation arises, while the matter remains active or any available process remains pending. All available processes — including negotiation, formal demand, and arbitration — must be fully exhausted before the engagement may be deemed concluded. No refund obligation arises while any such process remains pending or available.
(d) Term and Renewal. The initial term of this Agreement is two (2) years from the execution date. Provided Pioneer Legal Consulting has begun and continues to perform the agreed services and the matter has not been resolved, this Agreement shall automatically renew in successive twelve (12)-month increments at no additional charge to Client, until Resolution is achieved or all available processes are exhausted. Either party may decline renewal by providing written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term, provided that no renewal may be declined by Pioneer Legal Consulting while an active process remains pending.
